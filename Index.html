How is SGPA calculated?

SGPA is calculated using the credit-weighted average of grade points.

SGPA = Σ (Credit × Grade Point) ÷ Σ Credits

How is CGPA calculated?

CGPA is calculated using the credit-weighted average of the semester performance.

CGPA = Σ (SGPA × Semester Credits) ÷ Σ Semester Credits
